Search Engine Visibility

Getting FURD BASE on page one of Google for “architect Abuja”, “luxury home builder Nigeria”, and your own brand name.

Already Done

  • XML Sitemap generated — /sitemap-index.xml
  • robots.txt allows all crawlers
  • JSON-LD structured data (Organization + two addresses)
  • Canonical URLs on all 8 pages
  • Open Graph + Twitter Card meta tags
  • 8 pages indexed-ready (home, about, services, blog × 5)

Google Search Console Setup

  1. Go to search.google.com/search-console
  2. Add property: https://furdbase.ng
  3. Verify ownership (DNS TXT record or HTML file upload)
  4. Submit sitemap: https://furdbase.ng/sitemap-index.xml
  5. Check indexing status after 24-48 hours

Google Business Profile

This is how you appear on Google Maps and local search results. Without it, you will not rank for “architect Abuja” geographically.

  • Go to google.com/business
  • Register FURD BASE with your Abuja office address
  • Select category: Architect or Architecture Firm
  • Add phone, website, and 5+ project photos
  • Request 5 reviews from friends/clients
  • Post a weekly update (project progress, blog link, or photo)

Business Directories (Nigeria)

Get listed on these free directories to build backlinks and appear in local searches:

  • VConnect (vconnect.com)
  • Nigeria Business Directory (businesslist.com.ng)
  • Hotfrog Nigeria
  • Yellow Pages Nigeria
  • Finelib.com

Use consistent NAP (Name, Address, Phone) across all listings.

Content Engine

Blog posts, project updates, and on-site content that drives organic traffic and gives you ammunition for LinkedIn, WhatsApp, and email.

Already Done

  • Blog system with Astro Content Collections
  • 4 published posts on architecture, engineering, construction
  • Blog listing page + individual post pages with prev/next nav
  • About page + Services page with detailed content

Content Cadence

2 × week Blog post — 800-1,000 words, targeting one keyword. Include images from the project gallery. Monthly Project Matrix update — Add a new project or update an existing one with fresh photos. Quarterly Review Search Console — Check which keywords are ranking, adjust content strategy.

Blog Topics to Write Next

  1. “How Much Does It Cost to Build a Luxury Home in Abuja in 2026?” — Targets high-intent searchers
  2. “The Best Neighborhoods in Abuja for Luxury Real Estate Development” — Local SEO gold
  3. “Architect vs. Engineer: Who Do You Need First?” — Educational, shareable
  4. “Why Dubai Real Estate Investors Are Looking at Nigeria” — Bridges your UAE & Nigeria narrative
  5. “5 Buildings Defining Modern Nigerian Architecture” — Listicle format, highly shareable

How to Add a New Blog Post

  1. Create a .md file in src/content/blog/
  2. Add frontmatter: title, description, pubDate, image, tags
  3. Write the body in Markdown
  4. Rebuild: npm run build
  5. Post link on LinkedIn, WhatsApp, and include in next email
Every blog post is a piece of SEO fuel AND a LinkedIn post AND a WhatsApp share. One write-up, three distributions.

One-Week Launch Sprint

The first 7 days to get the flywheel spinning. Do these in order.

Day 1

Foundation Setup

Create Google Business Profile. Submit sitemap to Google Search Console. Complete LinkedIn Company Page fields.

Day 2

First LinkedIn Post + Network

Publish your first post (project spotlight with image). Send 20 connection requests to real estate/architecture professionals in Abuja.

Day 3

Publish Blog Post

Write and publish a new blog post (800-1,000 words). Share it on LinkedIn immediately after.

Day 4

Activate Your Network

Ask 3 friends to: (1) leave a Google review, (2) share the site in one WhatsApp group, (3) connect on LinkedIn and engage with your posts.

Day 5

Deepen LinkedIn

Connect with 20 more people. Engage with 10 posts (like + comment). Join 2 LinkedIn Groups.

Day 6

Publish Blog Post + Directory Listings

Publish blog post #2. Register on 3 Nigerian business directories.

Day 7

Review & Plan

Check Google Search Console — verify pages are indexed. Check LinkedIn analytics. Plan next week’s 2 blog posts. Check KPI dashboard.
