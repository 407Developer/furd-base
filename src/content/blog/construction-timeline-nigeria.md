One of the most common questions we hear at FURD BASE is: *"How long will my project take?"* The answer depends on the scope, site conditions, and procurement strategy. Here's a realistic, phase-by-phase breakdown for a typical luxury residential project in Abuja (400-600m²).

## Phase 1: Pre-Construction (2-4 Months)

Before a single shovel hits the ground, significant work must be completed.

| Activity | Duration | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Site survey & geotechnical investigation | 2-3 weeks | Borehole drilling, SPT, lab testing |
| Architectural design development | 4-8 weeks | Schematic design → design development → construction documents |
| Structural engineering | 3-4 weeks | Parallel with architectural CD phase |
| Approvals & permits | 4-8 weeks | Abuja FCDA, Abuja Municipal Area Council |
| Contractor procurement | 2-4 weeks | Tender, evaluation, negotiation |

**Total: 8-16 weeks**

## Phase 2: Site Preparation & Foundation (2-3 Months)

- Site clearing, demarcation, and temporary facilities: 1 week
- Earthworks (cut/fill, compaction): 2-3 weeks
- Foundation excavation: 1-2 weeks (depending on type — raft vs. piled)
- Reinforcement, formwork, and concrete pour: 3-4 weeks
- Curing and waterproofing: 2 weeks

**Total: 8-12 weeks**

*In Abuja's two rainy seasons (April-July, September-October), expect delays. Smart planning schedules foundation work for the dry season.*

## Phase 3: Structural Frame (3-5 Months)

- Ground floor columns, beams, slab: 3-4 weeks per floor
- Upper floors: 2-3 weeks per floor (post-tensioned slabs cure faster)
- Roof structure: 2-4 weeks

For a 2-storey luxury residence with a roof terrace, budget **12-16 weeks**.

## Phase 4: Building Envelope (2-3 Months)

- External walling (sandcrete blocks or precast panels): 3-4 weeks
- Window and door installation: 2-3 weeks
- Roofing and waterproofing: 2-3 weeks
- External rendering and cladding: 3-4 weeks

**Total: 10-14 weeks**

## Phase 5: MEP & Interiors (4-6 Months)

This is the longest phase and where most delays occur:

1. Rough-in MEP (conduits, piping, ductwork): 4-6 weeks
2. Plastering and screeding: 3-4 weeks
3. Tiling, stonework, and joinery: 6-8 weeks
4. Kitchen and wardrobe installation: 3-4 weeks
5. Painting and wall finishes: 3-4 weeks
6. Lighting, switches, and fixtures: 2-3 weeks
7. Flooring (engineered wood, marble, epoxy): 3-4 weeks

**Total: 16-24 weeks**

## Phase 6: Finishing & Handover (1-2 Months)

- Landscaping and external works: 3-4 weeks
- Snagging and defects rectification: 2-3 weeks
- Final cleaning and handover: 1 week

**Total: 6-8 weeks**

## Overall Timeline Summary

| Project Type | Estimated Duration |
|---|---|
| Luxury residence (400-600m²) | 12-16 months |
| Luxury duplex (600-1000m²) | 16-20 months |
| Mixed-use commercial (1000-3000m²) | 18-24 months |
| High-rise residential (8+ floors) | 24-36 months |
