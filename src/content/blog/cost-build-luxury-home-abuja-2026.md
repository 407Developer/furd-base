Building a luxury home in Abuja is one of the most significant investments you'll make. Whether you're constructing in Maitama, Asokoro, Jabi, or Katampe, understanding the true cost — not just the headline figure — is critical to avoiding budget overruns.

Here's what the numbers actually look like in 2026.

![Modern luxury residence in Abuja](/assets/blog/luxury-home-2.jpg)

## The Short Answer

For a luxury residence in Abuja (400-600m²), expect to spend between **₦250 million and ₦500 million** all-in. That includes design, construction, MEP systems, finishes, and landscaping.

The range is wide because "luxury" means different things to different people. A well-appointed 4-bedroom duplex in Katampe Extension sits at the lower end. A smart-home-enabled mansion in Maitama with imported Italian marble and floor-to-ceiling glazing sits at the upper end.

Here's where the money actually goes:

| Foundation & structural frame | 25-30% | ₦65M - ₦140M |

## What Drives the Price Up

These are the decisions that move you from ₦250M to ₦500M:

**Structural choices:** A piled foundation in Jabi's soft soil costs more than a raft foundation on the rock in Maitama. A post-tensioned slab system adds ₦15-25M but gives you larger column-free spans — worth it for open-plan living areas.

**Glazing:** Imported Low-E glass from Germany or the UAE runs ₦45,000-₦80,000 per m². Local alternatives exist at ₦20,000-₦35,000 per m², but thermal performance differs significantly in Abuja's heat.

**Smart home systems:** A full Crestron or Savant integration (lighting control, climate, security, audio) adds ₦20-40M. A basic automation setup (smart locks, CCTV, pre-wired speakers) runs ₦5-10M.

**Kitchen:** A custom Italian kitchen (Snaidero, Poliform) with Miele appliances can hit ₦30-50M. A high-quality Nigerian-made kitchen with imported appliances runs ₦8-15M.

## What You Should NOT Skimp On

Some things seem like easy places to save money, but they cost you later:

- **Structural engineering:** Don't let a contractor talk you out of proper reinforcement detailing. Abuja's laterite soils and seasonal flooding demand rigorous engineering.
- **Waterproofing:** Basement and bathroom waterproofing failures are the #1 cause of post-handover disputes. Use proven systems (Triton, Delta MS).
- **Electrical capacity:** Future-proof your distribution board. Running out of circuits in year 3 means tearing up walls.
- **Drainage:** Abuja's stormwater management is not optional. Your site drainage must handle peak rainfall events.
